Climate & Geospatial Data Scientist | Earth System Modeling & Machine Learning

Land surface properties shape weather, climate, and agriculture—yet most Earth system models treat a forest like asphalt. I develop physics-informed machine learning frameworks that teach models to recognize real-world complexity: how crops alter surface reflectivity seasonally, how snow interacts with vegetation, how local heterogeneity cascades into regional prediction skill.

My research integrates satellite observations, large-scale geospatial data processing, and deep learning to improve climate model representations across subseasonal to decadal timescales. I am committed to translating research into operational products that inform agricultural decision-making and climate services.

Research Focus

Land Surface Albedo

Most climate models average surface properties over 10 km grid cells—missing fine-scale heterogeneity. I develop ML frameworks that resolve subgrid albedo variability using satellite observations, then validate improvements in coupled model forecasts.

Crop Phenology Prediction

Planting date decisions affect crop exposure to drought and heat stress. I link satellite-observed leaf area index to growing degree days and albedo evolution, creating decision-relevant phenology forecasts for the DAWN agricultural support system.

Climate-Health Forecasting

Disease outbreaks follow meteorological patterns with lag times of weeks. I apply LSTM and time-series decomposition methods to identify climate drivers of disease incidence and build seasonal risk forecasts for multi-country health networks.

Air Quality & Attribution

Surface PM2.5 varies dramatically across regions and seasons. I conducted the first pan-India characterization study using concentration-weighted trajectories to quantify how distant emission sources impact local air quality.
