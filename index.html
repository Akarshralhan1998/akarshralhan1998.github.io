About

I am a PhD candidate in Atmospheric and Oceanic Science at the University of Maryland, specializing in land surface processes, remote sensing, and Earth system science. My research bridges physical understanding with machine learning to advance climate predictability, improve model representations of critical Earth system components, and reduce uncertainties in climate projections.

I develop physics-informed machine learning frameworks to capture spatiotemporal variability in land surface properties (particularly albedo) and integrate these advances into operational Earth system models for improved weather and climate prediction across regional to global scales and subseasonal to decadal timescales.

My technical expertise spans multi-method statistical modeling and large-scale geospatial data processing using Python (Xarray, pandas, scikit-learn, Dask), with rigorous uncertainty quantification, model validation, and bias correction of high-resolution climate datasets. I design robust, reproducible analytical pipelines using high-performance parallel computing and version-controlled workflows, enabling scalable delivery of transparent climate products for operational and research applications.

I am committed to bridging scientific research and operational impact through peer-reviewed publications, technical reporting, interdisciplinary collaboration, and effective communication of complex climate science to support climate services and informed decision-making.

Research Focus Areas

Global Land Surface Albedo Parameterization

Developing hybrid physics-informed ML frameworks to capture spatiotemporal and subgrid variability in land surface albedo. Integration with coupled models (CWRF) demonstrates improved surface energy exchange for weather and climate prediction.

Crop Phenology & Agricultural Decision Support

Building ML frameworks for crop phenology prediction and plantation date optimization. Established quantitative linkages between growing degree days, crop phenology, and albedo evolution for five major crops at 1km resolution.

Climate-Health Prediction Systems

Developing multinational climate-health forecasting frameworks linking meteorological variability with disease incidence. Applied advanced time series methods (ARIMA, EEMD, STL) and deep learning (LSTM, Neural Prophet) for seasonal health risk prediction.

Air Quality & Atmospheric Composition

Pan-India PM2.5 characterization and source apportionment using concentration-weighted trajectory analysis. Integrated meteorological and air quality datasets to assess environmental drivers of air quality variability.
