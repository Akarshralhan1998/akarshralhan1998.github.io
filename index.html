About

I am a climate and geospatial data scientist specializing in land surface processes, remote sensing, and Earth system modeling. My research bridges physical understanding with operational decision-support tools, focusing on translating complex climate-system interactions into actionable climate services.

I develop physics-informed machine learning frameworks to improve climate model representations and subseasonal-to-decadal predictions. Current work focuses on global land surface albedo parameterization for Earth system models, with applications to regional weather, seasonal forecasting, and decadal prediction skill. My methodological expertise spans memory-optimized geospatial data handling (50+ TB datasets), explainable AI, time series analysis, and high-performance computing workflows.

I am committed to producing reproducible, operationally relevant research products and communicating climate science through peer-reviewed publications, conference presentations, and compelling visualizations tailored to diverse stakeholder needs.

Research

Global Land Surface Parameterization

Developing hybrid physics-informed machine learning frameworks to capture spatiotemporal and subgrid variability in land surface albedo for Earth system models. Integration with WRF-based workflows for operational deployment at NOAA and NASA.

Agricultural Decision Support & Crop Phenology

Building machine learning frameworks for crop phenology prediction and planting date optimization. Operationalized within DAWN decision support dashboard for climate-resilient agricultural planning.

Climate-Health Prediction Systems

Quantifying climate-disease relationships through advanced time series analysis and deep learning (LSTMs, Neural Prophet) to enable early warning systems for disease outbreaks.

Air Quality & Atmospheric Composition

Pan-India PM2.5 characterization, source apportionment analysis, and application of concentration-weighted trajectory methods to identify dominant emission sources and sectoral contributions.

Keywords: Physics-informed ML, XGBoost, LSTMs, Explainable AI (SHAP, Counterfactuals), Xarray, GeoPandas, Dask, MPI, WRF, Subseasonal-to-Decadal Prediction, Crop Phenology, Climate Services
