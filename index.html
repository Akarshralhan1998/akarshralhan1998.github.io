About

I am a climate and geospatial data scientist specializing in land surface processes, remote sensing, and Earth system modeling. My research bridges physical understanding with operational applications in climate prediction and agricultural decision support.

I develop physics-informed machine learning frameworks to improve climate model representations and subseasonal-to-decadal predictions. Current work focuses on global land surface albedo parameterization and its implications for regional climate variability.

I am committed to producing reproducible, operationally relevant research products and communicating climate science through peer-reviewed publications, conference presentations, and community engagement.

Research

Global Land Surface Parameterization

Developing hybrid physics-informed machine learning frameworks to capture spatiotemporal and subgrid variability in land surface albedo for Earth system models. Integration with WRF-based climate modeling systems.

Agricultural Decision Support & Crop Phenology

Building machine learning frameworks for crop phenology prediction and planting date optimization. Operationalized within DAWN decision support dashboard for climate-resilient agricultural planning.

Climate-Health Prediction Systems

Quantifying climate-disease relationships through advanced time series analysis and deep learning (LSTMs, Neural Prophet) to enable early warning systems for disease outbreaks.

Air Quality & Atmospheric Composition

Pan-India PM2.5 characterization, source apportionment analysis, and application of concentration-weighted trajectory methods to identify dominant emission sources and sectoral contributions.

Keywords: Physics-informed ML, XGBoost, LSTMs, Explainable AI (SHAP), Xarray, GeoPandas, Dask, WRF, Subseasonal-to-Decadal Prediction, Crop Phenology, Climate Services
