Research

Physics informed machine learning for Earth system science

My research focuses on improving the representation of heterogeneous land surface processes while preserving physical interpretability, computational scalability, and usefulness for climate services.

01 · PHYSICS

Machine learning should remain grounded in physical processes

Build models around physically meaningful predictors and test whether their learned relationships remain consistent with established land surface behavior. SHAP, partial dependence, and counterfactual analysis provide complementary views of model sensitivity, helping distinguish physically plausible relationships from statistical associations that may not generalize.

02 · SCALE

Large scale data should not erase local information

Design memory efficient geospatial workflows that can process more than 50 TB of satellite and reanalysis data while retaining the spatial and temporal structure required for robust model development. Computational scalability should make higher resolution information usable, rather than forcing scientific questions to conform to computational limitations.

03 · APPLICATION

Research should become information that can be used

Move beyond model development by translating scientific outputs into information that supports agricultural planning, climate services, and Earth system model applications. The objective is to connect improved physical representation with decisions, forecasts, and modeling systems where better information can have practical value.

Selected projects

In a Nutshell: What I'm Doing

A concise view of the research questions I am currently pursuing and the problems I have worked on across land surface processes, climate services, and atmospheric science.

01 · PhD research

Global Land Surface Albedo Parameterization

Develop a physics informed machine learning parameterization that represents direct and diffuse land surface albedo across diverse land cover types while preserving the spatial variability needed by Earth system models. The work separates dynamic environmental variability from persistent subgrid heterogeneity, creating a scalable representation for weather, subseasonal to seasonal, and climate applications.

02 · Current research

Snow and Land Surface Albedo Dynamics

Extend land surface albedo parameterization to snow affected conditions by characterizing how snow fraction, surface state, and seasonal evolution alter visible and near infrared albedo. The objective is to develop a physically informed representation of snow albedo variability that improves the representation of surface energy balance and snowmelt related processes in land and climate models.

Snow albedoRemote sensingSurface energy balance
03 · Agricultural decision support

Crop Phenology and Planting Date Prediction

Connect crop phenology, growing degree days, leaf area index, and land surface albedo to improve the representation of crop development and surface energy exchange. This work translates climate and land surface information into decision support for agricultural planning across the contiguous United States and contributed to the DAWN climate services framework.

LAIGDDCONUSDAWN Dashboard ↗
04 · Climate and health

Climate Driven Disease Forecasting

Examine how temperature and precipitation variability influence disease incidence across diverse climatic regimes. The work combines temporal decomposition, statistical modeling, and recurrent neural networks to identify climate disease lag relationships and develop interpretable seasonal forecasting frameworks for public health applications.

Time seriesLSTMNeuralProphetClimate health
05 · Air quality and aerosols

PM2.5 Source Attribution Across India

Characterize the spatial and temporal variability of surface PM2.5 across climatically diverse regions of India and identify the meteorological and emission processes controlling that variability. The analysis integrates observations from 11 COALESCE sites with causality analysis, concentration weighted trajectories, emissions inventories, and HYSPLIT transport modeling.
